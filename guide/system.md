# Android Auto, the widget and the notification
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

## The notification and the lock screen

While an episode plays, Android's media notification and the lock screen show its title, show
and picture, with:

- play and pause,
- skip back and skip forward, by the seconds set in [Settings](settings.md#playback),
- the position bar, which you can drag.

Headphone and Bluetooth buttons send the same commands. The episode keeps playing with the app
in the background or the screen off.

## Android Auto

In the car, own.podcast shows:

- **New Episodes**: the episodes you can play now;
- **Downloads**: what is on the phone, newest first;
- **Shows**: your shows, each with its episodes;
- the chapters of the episode playing, when it has them.

The player in the car has the skip buttons and a **Speed** button that steps through 1×, 1.25×,
1.5×, 1.75×, 2× and 0.75×. You can also search by voice for a show. With nothing followed yet,
the car says **Follow a show on your phone**.

## The widget

Add the **Up Next** widget the usual way: touch and hold the home screen, tap **Widgets**, and
find own.podcast. It comes in a small and a wider size.

It shows the episode you are in, then episodes you have started, then the newest in New
Episodes: one on the small widget, up to three on the wider one. Tap an episode to play it.
With nothing to show, it says **Nothing new yet**.

## The shortcut

Touch and hold the own.podcast icon for **Continue Listening**, which goes on with the episode
you were listening to. You can drag it to the home screen as a shortcut of its own.

## Voice

Ask your phone's assistant to "play *show name* on own.podcast": it plays the episode you are in
for that show, or else its newest unplayed one. "Continue listening on own.podcast" goes on with
what you were listening to.

## In the background

About once an hour, when Android allows it, the app checks feeds and servers for new episodes,
so New Episodes and the widget are current when you look, and a show's download rules can fetch
the newest episodes. Android decides when this runs; battery saving can delay it. While the app
is open it checks again every 15 minutes.
